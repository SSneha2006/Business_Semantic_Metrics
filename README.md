
# Business Intelligence Semantic Layers With Metric Definition Consistency

## Project Overview

Business Intelligence Semantic Layers provide a centralized way to define and manage business metrics consistently across different departments.

In organizations, the same business metric, such as Revenue, may be calculated differently by Sales, Finance, and Marketing teams. This project focuses on maintaining consistent metric definitions and improving the reliability of business intelligence reports.

## Objectives

- Provide centralized business metric definitions.
- Maintain consistency across departments.
- Reduce differences in metric calculations.
- Improve accuracy and reliability of BI reports.
- Support better business decision-making.

## Problem Statement

Different departments may use different formulas and interpretations for the same business metric. This creates inconsistent reports and confusion during business analysis.

The proposed system aims to address this issue through a centralized semantic layer that maintains standardized metric definitions.

## Key Features

- Centralized metric definitions.
- Consistent business terminology.
- Standardized metric calculations.
- Improved reporting consistency.
- Better understanding of business metrics.
